5. KEY FEATURES
================================================================================

  A. Authentication & Security
     - Session-based authentication with Werkzeug password hashing
     - CSRF protection via @csrf_protected decorator
     - Login required and role required decorators
     - Self-demotion and self-deactivation safeguards for admins

  B. Product Catalog
     - Full CRUD: Name, SKU, Category, Price, Unit, Min Stock Threshold
     - Low stock threshold alert badges
     - Live client-side instant search and column sorting
     - Live Stock Preview Widget for projected balance calculation

  C. Inventory Transactions
     - Stock In  : Record incoming supplier inventory
     - Stock Out  : Record outgoing stock shipments
     - Adjust Stock : Audit reconciliations (damaged, lost, corrections)
     - Full searchable transaction audit log

  D. POS Billing
     - Cart management with auto-calculated subtotal, tax, discount, total
     - Thermal / A4 receipt view with page-width scaling
     - Bill history with re-print capability

  E. Export Service (export_service.py)
     - Excel: Auto column-width, styled headers, freeze panes
     - PDF: High-resolution A4, dynamic "Page X of Y" via two-pass rendering

  F. User Management
     - Admin panel: Register, activate/deactivate accounts
     - Role Requests Inbox: Review, approve, or reject elevation requests
     - Role Updation modal with live privilege description preview
     - My Profile: Edit name/email, change password, submit role requests
